  <h1>**Pizza order trends at Pizza 5PS in 2025**<h1>
</div>

**Background**

Pizza 5Ps was founded in 2025 as a Ho Chi Minh City-based restaurant specializing in pizza. At its launch, Pizza 5Ps offered a total of 32 different pizza flavors and 5 distinct sizes: S, M, L, XL, and XXL.

**Objectives**

This analysis focuses on data visualization to help Pizza 5Ps gain deeper insights into customer dining trends. Specifically, it highlights the most popular pizza flavors, revenue generated by each item, preferred pizza sizes, and peak sales periods.
